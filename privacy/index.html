Privacy Policy

Click Dare: Bait or Not? · Effective October 24, 2026

Click Dare is published by Charbel Nasrani. This policy covers the Click Dare app for iPhone and iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. Click Dare does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information.

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.
  • The app makes no network requests. It works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your settings (such as volume) and your play history are saved only on your device, so they are there next time you open the game. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated before the change reaches the app.
