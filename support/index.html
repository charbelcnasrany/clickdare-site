Click Dare: Bait or Not?

Found a bug, or have a question? Email us:

support@clickdare.app

Tell us your device and, if it's about a level, the level number and what the instruction said.

I died. Where did my progress go?

Every death deals a fresh, randomized run, so no two runs are the same. Once you reach level 7, deaths send you back to level 7 instead of level 1. That checkpoint lasts until you close the game.

Why doesn't the game tell me why I died?

On purpose. The instruction never lies, and the answer was in it. Read it again next time.

How do I play in Arabic?

Tap العربية at the top of the main menu. The game always starts in English.

Does the game collect my data?

No. Nothing leaves your device. See the privacy policy.
